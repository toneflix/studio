# Playground

Four made-up stems to play with. Drag clips by their body, pull their edges to trim, use the corner handles for fades, and drag below the last track to make a new one. With the timeline focused, <kbd>S</kbd> splits, <kbd>Delete</kbd> removes and <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>/<kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> undoes.

Press Play or Space to listen. Edits are heard straight away while it plays, and the M and S buttons mute and solo a track. Recording comes in the next release.
